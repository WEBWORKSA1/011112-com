What does 011112 mean?

Read it as 0 → 1111 → 2. You start from zero, pass four "1" milestones — Validate, Build, Launch, Earn — and arrive at 2: scaling by doubling what works. It also nods to binary (the 0s and 1s behind every AI and digital business) and to 11.11, the world's biggest online shopping day.

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every calculator and generator runs in your browser, needs no account and stores nothing on our servers. The site is supported by advertising and by readers.
