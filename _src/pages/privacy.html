Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information 011112.com ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information you give us

When you submit a form (launch plan, contact, newsletter, contest entry, job application, sponsorship or support pledge) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message. Forms are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-relay service (FormSubmit). We use this information only to respond to you, provide what you requested, run contests and, where you consented, send emails. You can unsubscribe at any time.

2. Tools and local storage

Our calculators and generators run entirely in your browser. Values you enter are not sent to us unless you choose to submit them in a form. Some tools save preferences (theme, checklist progress, saved names) in your browser's local storage; you can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform where required, and can change their choices at any time.

4. Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you click play. Once loaded, YouTube may collect data under Google's privacy policy. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

5. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate usage (pages viewed, device type). We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were sent (generally up to 24 months; job applications up to 12 months) unless the law requires longer.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data and to object to or restrict processing. Send a request through our contact form (topic: Privacy request).

8. Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. Contests are open only to people aged 18 or over.

9. Security and transfers

The site is served over HTTPS. Service providers may process data in other countries; we rely on their contractual and legal safeguards.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

11. Contact

Questions? Use our contact form.
