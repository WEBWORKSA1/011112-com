Why 11.11 matters to founders everywhere

November 11 — written 11.11 — began as Singles' Day among students in China and was turned into a shopping festival by Alibaba in 2009. It is now widely reported as the largest online shopping event in the world, and global marketplaces and brands increasingly run 11.11 promotions outside Asia. For a new venture, it's a ready-made reason to run an offer, and it kicks off the November–December peak that includes Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

How to use calendar moments

  • Six weeks out: pick the offer, build the landing page, warm your list.
  • Two weeks out: line up partners, schedule posts, test checkout.
  • On the day: send at the local start time, respond fast, extend only if you said you might.
  • After: onboard new customers well — event buyers churn fastest.
