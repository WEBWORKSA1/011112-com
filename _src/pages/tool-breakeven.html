The break-even formula

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost per unit). The bracket is your contribution margin: what each sale contributes toward fixed costs. Raise price or lower variable cost and break-even falls fast; it's often easier to improve margin than to find more customers.

For subscriptions, treat "units" as paying customers and price as monthly revenue per customer. Then use the MRR & LTV Projector to see how churn changes the picture, and read how to price a SaaS product.
