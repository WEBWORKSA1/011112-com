Reading your numbers

LTV here is average monthly revenue per customer divided by monthly churn — a simple revenue-based lifetime value. Multiply by your gross margin for a stricter version. An LTV:CAC ratio of 3:1 or better is a common benchmark for healthy acquisition; payback under 12 months keeps cash needs manageable.

Churn compounds. At 4% monthly churn you lose roughly 39% of a cohort in a year; at 2% it's about 22%. Halving churn is often worth more than doubling acquisition spend. Pair this with the Runway Calculator to see whether growth arrives before cash runs out.
