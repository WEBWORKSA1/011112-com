About 011112

A free launchpad for people turning ideas into businesses — built around a number that already tells the story.

The number

Read 011112 as 0 → 1111 → 2. Zero is where every venture begins. The four ones are the milestones nearly every successful business passes — validate, build, launch, earn. Two is the reward: doubling what works. We turned that sequence into the 0‑1111‑2 Method and built every tool on this site around it.

Why these digits resonate

  • Digital & AI. Zeros and ones are the binary code beneath every computer, AI model and online business.
  • Innovation. "Zero to one" is common startup shorthand for creating something genuinely new rather than copying what exists.
  • Commerce. 11.11 — Singles' Day — has grown into the world's largest online shopping event, a reminder that timing matters.
  • People. One is the solo founder; two is the partnership, the second hire, the first sign of scale.

Who it's for

First-time founders, solo builders, side-hustlers, students and small-business owners who want clear numbers and a clear sequence — not hype.

How we create content

Our guides are written and reviewed by the 011112 editorial team, drawing on hands-on experience building and monetising websites and online businesses. We cite well-known public frameworks where relevant, update articles when facts change, and label all sponsored content. Calculators show their formulas so you can check the math yourself.
