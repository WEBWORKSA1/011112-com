How revenue multiples work

Buyers and investors often value early-stage and small online businesses as a multiple of revenue (or of profit, for smaller cash-flow businesses). Recurring, high-margin, fast-growing revenue earns higher multiples; one-off, low-margin or high-churn revenue earns lower ones. This estimator starts from a baseline multiple per model and adjusts for growth, margin and churn.

Levers that raise your multiple

  • Lower churn and higher net revenue retention
  • Faster, more predictable growth
  • Higher gross margin
  • Diversified customers and channels
  • Clean books and documented processes
