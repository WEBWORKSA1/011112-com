What makes a business name work

A strong name is easy to say, easy to spell after hearing it once, and available as a domain and social handle. Short beats clever: names under ten characters are easier to remember and type. Avoid hyphens and numbers that people can't guess from hearing the name — unless, like a premium numeric domain, the number is the brand.

A five-point name test

  1. Radio test: say it aloud — can a listener spell it?
  2. Domain test: is the .com (or your market's main ending) available or affordable?
  3. Trademark test: search your country's trademark database for conflicts in your class.
  4. Meaning test: check it isn't negative in your target languages.
  5. Room-to-grow test: will it still fit if you add products?

Our generator is a starting point. Always run a trademark search before you commit — read how to choose a business name and domain.
