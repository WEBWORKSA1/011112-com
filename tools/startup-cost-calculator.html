How to use your startup cost estimate

Split costs into one-time (things you pay once to open the doors) and monthly (what it costs to keep them open). The monthly number drives your runway, which is why the calculator multiplies it by the number of months you want to fund and adds a contingency buffer — first ventures almost always cost more than planned.

Ways to cut the number

  • Validate with a landing page before paying for development.
  • Use free tiers and pay-as-you-go APIs until usage justifies upgrades.
  • Trade equity or revenue share for specialist help only when it's truly critical.
  • Delay incorporation costs until you have paying customers — where local rules allow.
