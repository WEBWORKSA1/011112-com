    How the Idea Score works

    The score adds your ten ratings and converts them to a 0–100 scale. It is a structured self-assessment, not a prediction: its value comes from forcing you to be specific about each factor. Founders tend to rate their own ideas generously, so a useful habit is to ask two people who know your market to score it as well and compare.

    What the bands mean

    ScoreSignalNext step
    75–100StrongPre-sell with a landing page this week.
    55–74PromisingTen customer interviews before building.
    35–54RiskyReframe the customer or the problem.
    0–34RethinkKeep the insight, change the idea.

    The factors that matter most

    If you only fix three things, fix problem pain, willingness to pay and reachability. A painful problem that people already pay to work around, sold through a channel you can access cheaply, beats a large market with no clear way in. Read our 7-day validation guide for the exact process.
