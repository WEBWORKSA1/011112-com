Burn, runway and "default alive" explained

Net burn is expenses minus revenue each month. Runway is how many months your cash covers that burn. Because revenue and expenses both change, this calculator projects month by month instead of dividing cash by today's burn.

"Default alive" is a term popularised in startup circles: if nothing changes — no new funding, same growth rates — does the company reach profitability before its cash runs out? If the answer is no, you are "default dead" and need to cut costs, grow faster or raise money now, while you still have time to do it calmly.

Rules of thumb

  • Start fundraising or cost-cutting with at least 6–9 months of runway left.
  • Small changes in monthly growth rate move the break-even month dramatically — test 5%, 10% and 15%.
  • Expense growth quietly kills runway; review subscriptions every quarter.
