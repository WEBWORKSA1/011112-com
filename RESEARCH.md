# 011112.com — Research & Concept Decision

## 1. What "011112" (0 · 1111 · 2) signals

| Lens | Finding | Use on the site |
|---|---|---|
| **AI / digital** | 0 and 1 are the binary alphabet of computing — every AI model and online business runs on them. | Binary-rain hero, "digital founder" positioning. |
| **Business / startups** | "Zero to one" (popularised by Peter Thiel's 2014 book *Zero to One*) is standard shorthand for creating something genuinely new (0→1) vs copying (1→n). | Core brand promise; referenced descriptively with a trademark disclosure. |
| **Commerce** | 11.11 (Singles' Day / Double 11) began among Nanjing students, was turned into a shopping festival by Alibaba in 2009 and is widely reported as the world's largest online shopping event; 2025 sales were reported above US$150B, exceeding Black Friday + Prime Day combined. | Commerce Launch Calendar tool + 11.11 guide; "1111" = four launch milestones. |
| **Economy / timing** | November 11 opens the Nov–Dec peak (11.11 → Black Friday → Cyber Monday → 12.12). | Calendar countdowns, seasonal sponsor takeovers. |
| **People / culture** | "1" = the individual/solo founder (the origin of Singles' Day symbolism); "2" = partnership, duplication, scale. Numerology treats 1 as initiation/leadership. | "Solo → scale" storytelling; contests and talent network. |
| **Date reading** | 01/11/12 = Jan 11 2012 / Nov 1 2012 — no dominant meaning; no major brand owns the string. | Low trademark-conflict risk; disclosure still added. |

**Synthesis:** read the domain as **0 → 1 → 1 → 1 → 1 → 2** = *Idea → Validate → Build → Launch → Earn → Scale*. That turns an arbitrary number into a memorable framework (the **0‑1111‑2 Method**) that every page, tool and CTA can hang on.

## 2. Monetisation logic (why this idea)

- **AdSense RPM:** business, finance, SaaS, AI, marketing and online-education are consistently among the highest-CPC AdSense verticals in 2026 industry roundups. A founder-tools site sits squarely in "business software / startup finance / AI tools" intent.
- **Evergreen calculator traffic:** cost, runway, break-even, MRR and valuation calculators attract repeat, high-intent searches and long time-on-page (good for ad viewability).
- **Lead value:** every tool funnels to a "free launch plan" form — leads for website builds, premium domains, AI automation and consulting (the owner's core business).
- **Extra revenue lines:** sponsorships (tool / newsletter / contest title), featured jobs, reader support, future affiliates.

Ideas considered and rejected: numerology/angel-number site (low CPC, thin-content risk for AdSense); generic date/countdown site (low RPM); binary-code converter (tiny CPC, crowded).

## 4. AdSense readiness checklist
- 33 pages of original content (8 long-form guides, method page, 9 tools with explanatory copy).
- Privacy policy with Google/third-party cookie language and opt-out links; terms; disclaimer; about (editorial policy); contact.
- ads.txt with `pub-6620975821265271`; Auto ads script in every `<head>`; manual slots configurable in `assets/js/config.js`.
- No prohibited content, no fake scarcity, no income guarantees; contest = skill-based, no purchase necessary.
- Clear navigation, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, mobile-responsive, fast static hosting.
